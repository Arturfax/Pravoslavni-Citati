Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up the lock screen widget?

Open the Pravoslavni Citati app and tap the "Widget Setup" button to see step-by-step instructions. On your lock screen, press and hold to customize, tap the + button, search for Pravoslavni Citati, and add your preferred widget size.

Why isn't my widget updating?

Widgets refresh at midnight automatically. Make sure:

  • The app has been opened at least once
  • You're running iOS 16 or later
  • Background refresh is enabled in Settings

What Bible translation is used?

Pravoslavni Citati features verses from the Orthodox Christian tradition in Serbian. The verses are selected to provide daily spiritual encouragement.

Is my data private?

Yes. Pravoslavni Citati does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, the app and widget work completely offline. You don't need an internet connection to view daily verses.
