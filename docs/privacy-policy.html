Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

Pravoslavni Citati ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Pravoslavni Citati is designed to minimize data collection. The App does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name or email address
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Personal device identifiers

How We Use Information

The App operates entirely on your device. Bible verses are stored locally and displayed based on the current date. No information is transmitted to external servers except as required by Apple's services.

iOS Widget Data

The Pravoslavni Citati lock screen widget operates locally on your device. Widget data is not sent to our servers or third parties. Apple may collect standard iOS analytics data through its operating system.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services. Your usage data is not shared with external parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is appropriate for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children. It contains no ads, no social features, and no tracking.
