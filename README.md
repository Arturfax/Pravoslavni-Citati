# ✝️ Serbian Orthodox Daily Verse (iOS)

An iOS application that displays a **daily verse from the Holy Bible** in the **Serbian Orthodox tradition**.

The app contains verses from the entire Bible and selects **one verse every day**, displaying it directly on your **Lock Screen** or **Home Screen widget** so users can reflect on Scripture throughout the day.

---

## 📖 Features

- **Daily Bible Verse**
  - A new verse is automatically selected every day.

- **Lock Screen Widget**
  - See the daily verse without unlocking your phone.

- **Home Screen Widget**
  - Keep Scripture visible throughout the day.

- **Complete Bible Verse Collection**
  - Verses are sourced from the full Bible.

- **Serbian Language**
  - Content is presented in Serbian.

- **Lightweight & Fast**
  - Widgets update automatically with minimal battery usage.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. The app stores a collection of Bible verses locally.
2. Each day a deterministic selection algorithm chooses the verse of the day.
3. The widget extension retrieves the daily verse.
4. Widgets update automatically on the **Home Screen** and **Lock Screen**.

This guarantees the verse remains **consistent throughout the day** across all widgets.
